FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about admin services, deliveries, and booking a run.

How long does a work permit application take in Cayman?

Processing times vary by job category, employer, and current government caseload, and can range from several weeks to a few months. We help make sure your application is complete and accurate the first time, which is one of the biggest factors in avoiding delays.

Can I get groceries delivered to Seven Mile Beach or other areas of Grand Cayman?

Yes. We handle grocery errands and deliveries island-wide, including Seven Mile Beach, West Bay, George Town, Bodden Town, East End, and North Side. Send your list and location on WhatsApp and we will confirm timing.

What areas of Grand Cayman do you serve?

We cover the whole island, from West Bay to East End, including George Town, Bodden Town, and North Side.

How do I book a Quick Run?

Send us a message on WhatsApp with what you need, or use the request form on this page. We confirm the details and get started, usually within minutes.

Do you help with business licensing and immigration paperwork?

Yes. Our admin services team prepares and submits work permit applications and renewals, trade and business licensing, and general immigration paperwork on your behalf.

What documents do I need for a work permit or immigration application?

Requirements vary by application type, but commonly include a valid passport, job offer or contract, employer information, and supporting government forms. We will tell you exactly what is needed for your specific application before you submit anything.

Is Quick Runs Cayman affiliated with the Cayman Islands Government?

No. Quick Runs Cayman is an independent, privately owned concierge and admin services company. We assist with preparing and submitting paperwork on your behalf, but all decisions are made by the relevant government department.
