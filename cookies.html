Cookie Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Quick Runs Cayman ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on www.quickruns.ky. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. They let a site remember information between page visits, such as a setting you have chosen.

Cookies we use

We use one essential, first-party cookie. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share cookie data.

Third-party services

Parts of this site load content from outside providers so the site can work:

  • Google Fonts - supplies the fonts used on the site. Your browser connects to Google to download them.
  • Supabase - stores and displays customer reviews and receives reviews you submit.
  • QR code service (api.qrserver.com) - generates the Instagram QR code shown on the homepage.
  • Vercel - hosts this website.

These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser connects to them. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, and Google Maps take you to those services, which apply their own cookie and privacy policies.

Managing cookies

You can view, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the essential cookie will not stop the site from working; the cookie notice will simply appear again on your next visit.

Changes to this policy

If we add new cookies, for example to measure site traffic, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.
